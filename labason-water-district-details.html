About This Application

Staff-facing billing console for Labason Water District: encode meter readings, post cash collections with franchise tax / WMMF / discounts, track leaking balances, and monitor arrears by aging. Dashboard KPIs cover total sales, customer count, leaking balances, and open tickets, with annual sales charts and customers-by-zone visualization. Labason uses a franchise-tax meter billing stack (distinct from Roxas VAT payment rules) and includes a maker–checker AR Adjustment module under Accounting.

  • Customers — listing by zone, add/edit accounts, Statement of Account (print/PDF)
  • Finance — meter readings, bills, cash payment, leaking entry & ledger
  • Accounting — AR Adjustment (credit notes, write-offs, debit memos → SOA + GL)
  • Reports — daily collection, monthly billing, low/no consumption, aging A/R, arrears & payment monitoring
  • Support — technical problems / message support; employees & role ACL
  • Ops — mobile notifications, SMS analytics, system activity, database backup

Stack, Tools & Architecture

  • App: labasonsandbox/ — CodeIgniter HMVC
  • Modules under application/modules/master/
  • UI: SmartAdmin 4 / Bootstrap 4 (sa4/) shell
  • Database: MySQL; Asia/Manila timestamps in footer
  • Billing: franchise tax on meter bills; WMMF, SC / leaking discounts
  • Reports: preview modal + Export to PDF / Excel / Print
  • Knowledge: Labason LWDKB (SA4, AR Adjustment, billing, ops)
